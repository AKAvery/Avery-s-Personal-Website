About Me Projects

I’m a second-year Computer Science student at the University of Texas at Austin, graduating in May 2028. This past summer I was a software engineering intern at Chinstrap Ventures, working on their Cossmology platform. I started this website to share my projects, keep track of what I’m learning, and build something that feels a little more personal than a resume.

When I’m not in class, I’m usually deep in a side project, playing tennis, hitting the gym, or picking up something new — piano, prediction markets, another language on Duolingo. I captained my high school tennis team at line #1 doubles and still play whenever I can. I also just love listening to music; it’s the background to pretty much everything I do.

On the tech side, I work mostly in Python and TypeScript. Lately that has meant async systems — a live algorithmic trading bot on Kalshi that has moved $3.6K+ in real volume — and full-stack products on Next.js and Supabase, including an AI rehab platform with 256 API routes and 81 row-level-security migrations behind it. I spend a lot of time on LangGraph multi-agent pipelines and on how to actually evaluate them, which is what led to OrcaStrike, a forensic debugger that traces where trust breaks in a multi-agent workflow. I also won first place at the Longhorn Neurotech Fall Competition owning the Python-to-ESP32 networking layer for a gesture-controlled robot, and I got my start writing Java for FRC robotics. Every one of these pushed me somewhere new, which is the point.

Thanks for stopping by — feel free to click around the floating icons, or head to my projects to see the details. Adios!
